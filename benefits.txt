DECISION
Based on the household means-test conducted at your Social Service Office, your application for ComCare Short-to-Medium-Term Assistance is approved. Your household will receive monthly cash assistance of $1,150.00 for a period of 6 months, subject to the review described below.

ELIGIBILITY
